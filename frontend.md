FAQ Content
What does this website do?
 We compare beauty products across multiple online stores so users can find the best prices in one place.


How do you compare products?
 We collect product data such as price and availability from partner stores and display them side by side.


Are the prices accurate?
 Prices are updated regularly. Always confirm the final price on the store’s page before purchasing.


Which stores do you compare?
 We compare products from popular beauty stores including local and international retailers operating in Nepal.


Do you sell products directly?
 No. DealFinder does not sell products. We redirect users to the store.


How do you make money?
 We may earn a small commission when users purchase through our links at no extra cost.


How often are prices updated?
 Prices and availability are refreshed regularly.


Can I filter comparisons?
 Yes. Users can filter by brand, price range, ratings, and product type.


Why are some products missing?
 Some stores may not be integrated yet. We are continuously expanding.


Are reviews included?
 Ratings and review summaries are shown when available.


How do I search?
 Use the search bar or browse categories.


Can I suggest a store?
 Yes. Contact us to suggest stores or products.


Why are prices different?
 Prices vary due to promotions, shipping, and store policies.


Are shipping and taxes included?
 Base price is shown. Shipping and taxes depend on the store.


Is there a user account or wishlist?
 Yes. Users can create accounts to save products and track prices.
