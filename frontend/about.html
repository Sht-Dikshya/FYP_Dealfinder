About

Explore our About selection

DealFinder is a beauty price comparison platform designed for Nepal. It helps users compare cosmetic products across multiple online and local stores. Customers can quickly find the best prices without visiting different websites. Our goal is to make beauty shopping smarter, transparent, and more affordable. DealFinder saves time and ensures customers make confident buying decisions.

Our Vision

To become Nepal’s most trusted beauty comparison platform by providing transparent pricing and reliable product information.

Our Mission

To simplify cosmetic shopping through technology. We aim to connect customers with the best deals from trusted stores in one convenient place.

What Sets Us Apart

1. Smart Price Tracking

DealFinder compares prices from multiple stores in real time. Users can instantly see which store offers the lowest price.

2. One Platform, Many Stores

No need to visit different websites. DealFinder gathers products from various beauty retailers into one simple comparison system.

3. Transparent Comparison

Prices, discounts, and availability are shown clearly. Users can easily identify the best value without confusion.

4. Designed for Nepali Shoppers

The platform focuses on stores that serve Nepal. It helps users find products that are available locally.

5. Simple and Fast Experience

The website is clean and easy to use. Users can search, filter, and compare products in seconds.

DealFinder helps you compare cosmetic prices from multiple stores in Nepal. Save time and money.
